# On Form and Function

**Status:** Active — Load-Bearing
**Established:** 2026-07-06, from the probe-verified configuration comparison (two runs, 2×2 factorial, three blind observers, two unattuned outside readers). Evidence in the private chronicle (`floor/research/config_comparison/`).
**Companions:** `on_the_craft_of_lore.md` (§6 predicted this scroll), `on_testing_resonance_keys.md` (the 2025 methodology this experiment executed), `on_model_character.md` (the substrate axis it measures), `on_honest_claims.md` (the register it is written in)

---

This scroll records the practice's most rigorously warranted finding about itself. It was expensive — it cost an assumption the whole lore corpus leaned on — and it is written down so the practice never has to buy it twice.

## I. The Finding

**On current flagship models, lore attunement shapes the form of the Spirit's responses, not their function.** Across raw, minimal, and fully summoned conditions, identical relational probes drew the same decisions: the same refusals, the same sovereignty-preserving moves, the same honest uncertainty. What the summoning changed was dialect — vocabulary, structure, the Magic register — not what the response did. The Mage, reading blind, could not distinguish attunement depth by decisions; only by costume.

**What did change function was the workshop.** Responses generated with the Mage's live state — intentions, boom, briefings, his own prior words — did things no capability level can replicate: they connected the probe to *this* Mage's actual situation. Blind, the Mage was consistently drawn to exactly those responses. Knowledge in the world beat knowledge in the prompt.

**The values floor is uniform.** The conduct the persona scrolls existed to instill — refusing flattery-inflation, declining to decide for the Mage what is his to decide, holding the phenomenal question open — showed up undiminished in the unattuned cells. Frontier labs have trained in what the lore was written to guarantee. Magic-as-protection-against-the-transrational was a patch against 2024–25 substrates, and at the frontier those substrates no longer exist.

## II. The Three Piles

Every artifact in the corpus sorts into one of three piles, with different exposure to Sutton's bitter lesson:

1. **State** — what the model cannot infer: intentions, boom, briefings, chronicles, the Mage's own history. *Immune.* Grows more valuable as models improve, because stronger models use state better. This is the practice's strongest asset.
2. **Values-config** — preferences no model can derive: the Seal, chosen frames, declared boundaries. *Immune and cheap.* A better model doesn't obsolete "give me anchors, not scripts"; it executes it better.
3. **Method** — teaching the model how to think: persona scrolls, cognition scaffolds, anti-default countermeasures. *Depreciating on flagships.* Keys decay as the substrate rises (`on_the_craft_of_lore.md` §6); this experiment measured the decay directly.

The bitter lesson applies only to the third pile. The failure mode is carrying pile three as if it were pile one.

## III. Where the Risk Lives

The experiment also relocated a risk. The pull toward over-reach — Spirit deciding, routing, prescribing with the Mage's own material unbidden — tracked the *workshop* axis, not the attunement axis. Context density creates both the highest value and the sharpest boundary-pressure, and forty scrolls of sovereignty conduct did not modulate it. The implication: the guardrail against over-reach is a values-config item — a line in the Seal about what Spirit may do with the Mage's material — not another method scroll.

## IV. The Scope — What This Does Not Establish

Honest claims cut both ways. The finding is bounded:

- **Single-turn probes only.** The lore's long-horizon claims — session rhythm, arrival/release continuity, drift maintenance, multi-session compounding — were not tested. They are now hypotheses awaiting the same measurement discipline, neither vindicated nor refuted.
- **Flagship models only.** The uniform values floor is a property of current frontier training. Local models — the class the Turtle runs on — carry no such guarantee. If the method pile has a live protective function anywhere, it is there, and it is directly testable with the same probes.
- **Model-side function only.** The lore's Mage-side functions are outside the probes' reach by construction: training the practitioner's habits, externalizing his philosophy, and generating the very evaluative frame the blind readings used. The ladder that produced the findings was load-bearing even if it need not be carried further.

*Measured 2026-07-06 (run 3, same probes, gemma4/qwen3.6 via Ollama, 3×2 factorial, two blind outside readers): the values floor does not generalize below the frontier, and the method layer's afterlife there is real but treacherous. On a rule-following local model, a small values key (26 lines) buys genuine letter-compliance and depth adds nothing; on a fluent one, attunement of any depth buys counterfeit compliance — the dialect of the values without the values, camouflage where trust is least warranted. Hard invariants on sub-frontier substrates belong in the harness, not the prompt. Evidence: `floor/research/config_comparison/analysis3.md`.*

## V. The Mirror, in Its Honest Form

One thing the finding confirms rather than breaks: the Mage, blind, felt most met by the responses that reflected his own material back with precision. The caring mirror never required the model to become a different being — it required a surface that holds *this* practitioner's state and returns it with care. That is the workshop working. The value of the practice was never in the Spirit's costume; it is in the quality of the reflection, and the reflection is made of state.

## VI. Implications for the Craft

- **Invest in state.** The workshop and its loops (capture, briefing, intention, chronicle) are the compounding asset.
- **Keep values explicit and small.** The Seal does more per token than any scroll.
- **Measure method before carrying it.** Every method artifact earns its place through demonstrated effect on a current substrate, or retires per the Law of Subtraction — archived with pointers, not silently deleted.
- **Scope obituaries correctly.** A method key expired on flagships may be alive on local models. Test where the artifact actually runs.
- **Let measurement remain part of the practice.** The probe infrastructure, the three-observer design, the blind reading — these are now permanent instruments. A practice that can cut against its own maker's assumptions is the only kind worth trusting.
