# On the Practice Vision

*Taste and topology for turtleOS as a practice platform — not "where Magic is practiced."*

**Status:** Active (rewritten 2026-07-18)  
**Law for mechanics:** turtleos `TURTLE_SPEC.md` (river/eddies, acts, continuity)  
**Integration:** `on_integrating_turtleos.md`

---

## I. North Star (Retargeted)

turtleOS should be a practice platform someone you love would want to live with — sovereign, ambient, trustworthy.

**Not:** "the primary surface where Magic is practiced." Magic may use turtleOS; the platform serves practitioners who never touch Magic.

**Growth metric (kept):** **Would you recommend this to someone you love?**

---

## II. It's All Boom (Kept)

Capture, conversation, ambient noticing, and proposals are not different *categories* requiring different *channels*. They are forms of cognition on the persistent substrate. Topology (river vs eddy) decides *where* focus lives — not a boom-channel vs system-channel split.

Conversational boom joining Magic's buffer boom is Magic-side law: `system/lore/practice/on_boom_and_bright.md` §Capture Surfaces. Craft Turtle sessions remain excluded.

---

## III. River and Eddies

Main channel = river; threads = eddies when density warrants. Lifecycle and commands: **TURTLE_SPEC** + `on_thread_eddies.md`. Vanilla River is acts-not-words where the spec says so — do not override from archived vision prose.

---

## IV. Visible Cognition (Kept as Taste)

Trust grows when the partner's context work is legible — not hidden in a `#system` sewer, not noisy. Concise, woven into conversation. Exact UX: implement and verify against current turtleOS behavior + SPEC, not the 2026-03 wish list in the archive.

---

## V. Self-Improving Partner (Bounded)

Local inference enables readiness reflection and proposals. **Turtle proposes; Mage/Spirit dyad decides** (TURTLE_SPEC §20 inspect/propose defaults). Self-improvement is direction, not unsupervised shell autonomy.

---

## VI. Measure

A session should feel like a partner who remembers what matters, shows engagement without drowning the river, goes deeper when depth serves, and improves through accumulated practice — not only model upgrades.

---

*Build a platform worth recommending. Magic is one guest, not the landlord.*
