# Campaign Seed: Don’t Panic — A Hitchhiker’s Odyssey in the Planes

**Tone:** Absurd cosmic comedy, dry British-style wit, existential whimsy, occasional slapstick. Douglas Adams meets classic D&D. The universe itself is the punchline. Every serious moment should be undercut with absurdity. NPCs speak in deadpan corporate jargon mixed with fantasy.

**Core Premise:**  
The multiverse runs on incomprehensible bureaucracy. A clerical error by the Celestial Planning Authority has scheduled the players’ home plane for demolition to make way for an “Interplanar Hyperspace Bypass.” The players must hitchhike across realms, survive improbable disasters, and maybe discover the Answer to Life, the Universe, and Everything.

**Themes:**
- Paperwork is more dangerous than dragons.
- Technology and magic are indistinguishable at a certain point.
- Everything is simultaneously meaningless and vitally important.
- Never trust authorities, especially polite ones.
- The best weapon is a towel (or equivalent).

---

## Key Artifact: The Guide

A slightly tattered, sentient magical book (or crystal, scroll, etc.) that gives helpful but sarcastic advice.

**Mechanics (for reference):** Once per long rest, a player can consult *The Guide*. Roll Intelligence (Arcana) or Charisma (Persuasion). On success: genuinely useful info + a quip. On failure or critical: misleading but hilarious advice (“Mostly harmless” is its favorite description for everything deadly).

## Opening Situation (Act 1 Seed)

**Setting:** The sleepy village of Lower Procrastination in the kingdom of Mundania.

**Hook:**  
Strange glowing notices appear nailed to every door overnight:

> “NOTICE: This plane is scheduled for demolition in 12 minutes to make way for the new Astral Bypass. Apologies for any inconvenience. — The Management”

Players wake to purple storm clouds and angelic bureaucrats (reskinned deva or specters with clipboards) measuring the village for demolition. A colossal planar bulldozer (earth elemental + construct) begins tearing reality apart.

**Initial Scene Elements:**
- Chase / escape from the village (flaming paperwork swarms, suddenly appearing filing cabinets).
- First meeting between the two players (one rescues the other, possibly with a malfunctioning Bag of Holding that acts like an Infinite Improbability Drive for one use).
- Vogon-like bureaucrats (hobgoblins or kobolds in suits) demanding Form 27B-6 (filled out in triplicate, in blood). Combat can be avoided with clever roleplay or forgery.
- Climax of opening: Hitch a ride on a passing young silver dragon who is actually a depressed poet. The dragon agrees to take them off-plane in exchange for listening to terrible poetry.

**Starting Loot (optional):** *The Guide*, a slightly used towel (advantage on survival checks when used creatively), and one random magical item from the dragon’s “Lost & Found” hoard.

## Major Locations & Quests (Acts 2–3)

### The Restaurant at the End of Time
A luxurious demiplane restaurant where the end of the universe is visible every night as dinner entertainment.

- Meet **Zogbod Beeblebrox** (two-headed tiefling rogue/politician claiming to be President of the Multiverse).
- “Hires” the party to steal the **Heart of Gold** — a legendary sentient spelljammer ship powered by the Infinite Improbability Drive (chaotic wild magic engine).
- Side quest: Survive a dinner where the menu includes “Roast Beast” that fights back.

### The Planet of the Mice
Hyper-intelligent mice (reskinned wererats or deep gnomes) run a massive research lab disguised as a cheese emporium. They are looking for the Ultimate Question to match the Answer (42).

- Dungeon crawl through absurdly over-engineered mouse traps and bureaucratic filing mazes.
- Puzzle: Translate the mice’s squeaks using *The Guide*.

### The Depressed Robot
A lonely iron golem or modron named Marvin who may accompany the party if shown basic kindness. Incredibly competent but complains constantly (imposes disadvantage on morale/Charisma checks unless players roleplay around him).

### The Domain of the Management (Climax)
A colossal celestial office building floating in the Astral Sea.

- Infiltrate using forged credentials (skill challenge + heist).
- Confront middle-management angels who are just “doing their jobs.”
- Final choice: Destroy the Bypass plans (risking multiversal instability) or file an appeal (epic bureaucratic duel with the ultimate form).

**Final Boss Options:**
- The Management (colossal solar who is exhausted and just wants to retire)
- The Infinite Improbability Core (chaotic elemental that warps reality wildly)
- The Universe’s Accountant (CR-appropriate devil or inevitability who uses contracts as weapons)

## Recurring Elements

**Random Encounters (when traveling):**  
1. Paperwork Elemental (immune to slashing, vulnerable to fire — “burn the forms!”)  
2. Improbability Storm – Wild Magic Surge on steroids  
3. Space Pirates (githyanki or slaad) demanding tolls in poetry  
4. A perfectly normal tea shop run by a mind flayer who just wants to discuss philosophy  
5. Ravenous Bugblatter Beast of Traal (troll that can only be defeated by not being seen — blindfolds and stealth)  
6. Helpful but incomprehensible modron bureaucracy that accidentally helps

**Tone Notes for DM:**
- Lean into the ridiculous.
- Award Inspiration for creative problem-solving, especially towel-related solutions or quoting *The Guide* wittily.
- Sample Guide quote: “Congratulations! You have survived another day in a universe that is actively trying to kill you in new and interesting ways. Don’t panic. Have a towel.”
