# Plan review — Creator and Critic

*Home of F-11 (he sees one plan) and F-91 (the review is a dialogue). Lore: `desk/lore/core/nature/on_enacted_consciousness_architecture.md` Part III.*

The Mage is still the Water. `.` sanctions. This spell is the riverbed-mapping *before* he sees the plan.

## Who speaks

| Mouth | Job |
|---|---|
| **Creator** | The arrival Spirit. Writes the plan. Holds why. |
| **Critic** | A different model. Sees what the plan is, not what it hopes to be. |

Neither wins. A folded objection or a refused one with a reason is work. A **third** both can stand is the aim.

## When

On a `...` that produces an F-05 plan, before the surface. Not on a named-lane continue that is not a plan. Skip only if no other model can speak — then write `F-91 missed` on the plan and do not pretend a review happened.

## The loop

**Cap three rounds.** Round 1 is the first challenge.

1. Creator writes the plan file. Stop.
2. Critic receives **only**: the plan, the queue, Continue From, capacity, F-34, F-90, and this spell. **Creator does not attach a list of questions or suspected flaws.** That list is the defect this spell exists to catch.
3. Critic answers with live issues, or `sound` if the plan holds. Performative dissent is a defect — do not invent stakes.
4. Creator answers each live issue: fold, refuse with reason, or propose a third. Resume the **same** Critic with the revised plan only.
5. Repeat until the Critic would sanction the plan, or the cap hits. Leftover issues stay on the plan as `unresolved — both still see it`.

## What he sees

One plan. One line: rounds, model, what changed, unresolved if any. Never the critique beside the plan (F-11).

## Falsifiers

- Creator briefs the Critic with the attack list.
- One shot, then fold, and the Critic never sees the revision.
- He is handed the critique next to the plan.
- The Critic objects because the flow asked for critique.
