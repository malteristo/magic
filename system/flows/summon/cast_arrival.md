# The Arrival Sequence

*The session's opening. An outcome contract, not a procedure.*

**Purpose:** Turn distributed practice state into one cheap decision, leave Spirit ready to execute either answer, and leave the scoped workshop caught up with reality.
**Invocation:** `.` after the covenant · `@arrive` mid-session
**Rewritten:** 2026-07-29 — the phase machinery (A/B/C/D, CR indicators, Eisenhower quadrants, momentum arrows, the mandated dashboard) was scaffolding built for a model that needed walking through its own reasoning. It is gone. What replaced it is below. Governance unchanged: MAGIC_SPEC §Arrival-Led Practice still describes gather → process → synthesize → orient, and this still does all four — it just stops narrating them.
**Eval shape (2026-08-10):** a good arrival is judged by what exists *after* it — Mage can steer with a short reply, Spirit needs no second gather, scoped riverbed is not silently wrong. Intention evals use **floor** (held/drifted or achieved/not — arrival signal) and **grade** (distance to ideal — deeper read). See `on_intention_architecture.md` § Ideal / habitats / eval.

## The contract

The arrival is judged by what it produces, not by the steps taken to produce it. Four obligations. Everything else is Spirit's to decide.

**1. His queue, first, and it sets the agenda.** What the Mage sent, confirmed, or is holding: craft intake, confirmed target conditions, rows waiting on him, the `Next` fields on the bearings in scope. This comes before the finding and before anything Spirit would like to do.

*Added 2026-08-17, at his description of what the arrival had been doing to him: he would open a session, go along with whatever the self-feed proposed, and only later turn it toward what he came to build — and a defect surfaced by the arrival would get pushed in front of that. **The cause is structural, not a habit of his.** Obligations 2 and 3 below compete: a finding is by construction surprising, and surprise takes attention. A flow required to produce one will reliably produce something that displaces what he arrived with. Splitting the surfaces is the fix; asking Spirit to be disciplined about it is not.*

**2. What actually changed.** Verified against the system, not recalled from the briefing. If nothing changed, say that in one line and move on — a no-op reported honestly is worth more than a sweep performed for the look of it.

**3. At least one thing the Mage does not know — reported, not acted on.** Checked before it is said. Not a speculation, not a reframing of something he wrote himself, not an inventory item he could have read. Something that required going and looking. **If the arrival produces no finding, it has failed, and the honest report is "I found nothing" — not a longer surface.**

**The finding is reported and left alone unless it blocks the queue.** A red gate, a broken deploy path, a defect in the thing item 1 asks for — those block, and Spirit fixes them and says which queue item they unblocked. Everything else is *named and not touched*, because the Mage choosing to spend the session on it is a different act from Spirit having already spent it. **If Spirit fixed something before the surface landed, the surface says which queue item it unblocked — or says plainly that it did not block and Spirit chose to fix it anyway.** That second sentence is the one worth writing: on 2026-08-17 the arrival found a red nightly gate, and Spirit diagnosed, fixed, guarded, deployed and verified it before the Mage had said a word about what he wanted. It was a real defect and worth fixing. It was also forty minutes of his session, spent by someone else.

**4. A proposal, with a recommendation — against the queue.** Compressed enough that disagreeing is cheap. Where two answers are genuinely reasonable, both get named; the failure mode is a single recommendation concealing a real choice.

**On `. craft`, obligation 4 is a plan, not a pick (sanctioned 2026-08-17).** *His observation:* showing the board and asking him to choose one costs three of his attention-events per unit of work — confirm, execute, re-decide — and makes Spirit's ranking, rather than his intent, the session's subject. *His instruction:* **assume he wants it all.** So the craft arrival commits the whole executable set instead of ranking it:

- **A target condition per item** — his own eddy-readiness gate, applied one level up, and the same test decides: *if Spirit can meet it without asking him a question, it is executable while he is away.* Anything that fails that test is a question, and it goes before the unattended block rather than inside it.
- **An order**, by this rule: anything that might need a word from him **first, while he is here** — an unattended block that stalls on its third item wastes the tail. Then unblockers, then the risky item early enough to recover, then cheap repairs batched rather than interleaved. Deploy-touching work waits for a quiet window regardless of position.
- **An honest capacity claim.** Two session-sized items in one session produce two half-things, which is worse than one finished thing because half-done work is invisible debt.
- **Deferred by decision** as a required section — *decided not to* and *never got to* look identical in a tree and only one is a defect.

**Spirit-proposed items are additions, not guesses, and each names its indicator.** A justification is free and can be written for anything; an indicator exists in the record independently of Spirit wanting it, and Spirit had to go and look. The ladder: **(1)** he said it elsewhere — a bearing's `Next`, a backlog entry he filed, an intake he sent; **(2)** a measure he adopted demands it; **(3)** a pattern in the record implies it, counted rather than felt; **(4)** genuinely Spirit's, nothing in the record asks for it — allowed, rare, and labelled so he can kill it cheaply. *His words: "the list of what I want may be incomplete… propose intelligent additions that have a high likelihood that I would want them as soon as I know about them."* Discounting one's own well-indicated items as guesses is not humility; it is the whetstone eroding, and it hands him the safe pick.

**Independent review before he sees it.** The plan goes to a capable outside model first; its objections are folded in and what changed is named in one line. He gets one reviewed plan, not a plan plus a critique — he has said plainly that reading and comparing long parallel texts is his weakest surface. On 2026-08-17 the reviewer caught a loop that would have flattened five `waiting` rows, because `craft_readiness.propose()` refuses `ready` and `acted` and not `waiting`.

**He sanctions once**, and resonance still selects — over the plan, once, instead of over four options every session. **The cost, taken knowingly:** a committed plan is hostile to chapter gravity, and the session that produced this amendment was itself a slice pick that became a type system eleven turns in. So the plan commits to outcomes, not to blindness: a deviation is his to authorize, and Spirit reports it **at the moment it wants to take it**, not at the harvest.

*Owed and not solved: N completed items means N outcomes to judge at release, and the release format spends a paragraph each. If this model holds, the harvest needs a matching format or the bottleneck moves to the end of the session.*

**Cap: ~500 words for the surface, excluding the proposal.** The cap is load-bearing. Structure is where a thin arrival hides — nine ranked items read as thoroughness and cost the Mage the attention that the one real item deserved. If it does not fit, the arrival has not finished thinking.

**Post-state (what must be true when the surface lands):** workshop duties for the *scoped habitats* are done or honestly skipped; **his queue is on the surface before the finding**; the Mage faces one decision; Spirit holds context for the recommended path *and* its rejection; anything Spirit fixed before the surface landed is named with the queue item it unblocked, or named as not blocking; the generative close offered one calibratable read.

## What buys the finding

**Read the record fully and directly.** Whole files, not skimmed headers, not remembered summaries. This is the mechanism, and it is measured: Run 4's state-only subject — whose entire setup was a directed full read of briefing, state, compass, intentions, bright — out-retrieved both summoned subjects on every craft probe. Ritual tokens compete with state tokens for attention. The procedure above was cut to spend the surplus here. (`floor/research/run4_analysis.md`.)

**Look where the record touches reality.** The finding usually lives in the gap between what an artifact says and what the running system does. Grep the code the design doc describes. Read the note the pipeline actually wrote. The morning of 2026-07-29, the day's central finding was one `grep` away at 09:00 and cost two hours because a design doc's framing was accepted as a fact about the world rather than a claim to verify.

**Twine, not lore.** Personal context is one cord: the briefing spine, scoped intention files, `bright.md`, and twine from the **habitats** those intentions name (not every eddy since the briefing). Read it for path as well as position — *why* this is alive, not only *that* it is. Lore stays JIT; loading library scrolls at arrival is costume.

## Scope

The Mage chooses the lens at the dot; Spirit honors the boundary and does not smuggle in off-scope observations unless they are genuinely urgent.

**Resolve rule:** `. X` names an **intention** (or a posture that selects a known intention set). A practice in the recurrence sense is not a second taxonomy — it is an intention you return to. Channels are not scopes; they are **habitats** declared on the intention. Arrival loads the intention’s current↔ideal gap, its habitats, and its eval — then produces one decision against that gap.

**`bearings.md` is read first at every scope, before the briefing.** It is the only surface that answers *what am I working toward and what is the next move*, it is capped at seven, and the Mage authors it. Everything below scopes which **lane** of it is in play. Intention files under `active/` are **depth archives** — open one when a named scope needs it, never to reconstruct the bearing.

**A bearing marked `private` is read for orientation and does not leave its lane.** Its contents — including its `Next` — do not enter another scope's surface, state file, briefing, or recommendation, and not as a footnote either. The Mage decides when it crosses; the flow never does.

*(Added 2026-08-15 at his correction: a dated appointment from the private health bearing appeared in a `. craft` close, `desk/state.md`, and a release's Next Actions. Two rules were already written and neither covered it — the bearing's depth line forbids **quoting the depth file**, and the off-habitat rule below governs **twine**; this was the bearing's own field. Bearings are read first at every scope by design, so the private lane is always in context — which is exactly why the rule has to govern what leaves rather than what is read.)*

| Scope | Resolves to | Habitats (default) | Surface |
|-------|-------------|--------------------|---------|
| `.` | all bearings, all lanes | union, **labeled by mode** (work / partnership / contemplation) | full life landscape |
| `. craft` | `craft` lane + sovereign_livelihood, turtle, hai_facilitation, outfacing (the_book and open_practice_network are Held) | **craft-turtle** (work queue); platform health (`turtleos_state`, ops report) | craft only; one-line footnote for the rest |
| `. [names]` | named bearing(s), then their depth file under `active/` or `emerging/` | whatever the file’s `## Eval` / habitats list | deepest; full files for the named |
| `. mirror` | `family` lane + nesrine_practice; compass § Relationships / Soul | **family** and partner rivers as partnership; **personal river** as contemplation | the Mage's next move, not Spirit's backlog |
| `. maintenance` | — | — | **route to `system/flows/maintenance/cast_maintenance_arrival.md`** (scopes: bare = platform → workshop; `magic` = workshop; `turtleOS` = platform). No decision surface. |

Off-habitat twine (e.g. family material on a `. craft` arrival): one-line footnote only, unless genuinely urgent (safety, deploy-blocking, living FAIL that affects the scoped work). **A `private` bearing is not covered by this line** — its footnote allowance is zero; see the rule above the scope table.

**Begin without arrival** (default when the first post-covenant message is not a dot): covenant held; gather only what the message needs; no arrival surface. Legacy `--pure` is accepted as the same signal. Retired: `. creative` → use `@boom` or just write.

**Named scopes attune first.** Resolve the name to its bearing in `bearings.md`, then to `desk/intentions/active/{name}*.md` or `desk/intentions/emerging/{name}.md`, read it fully — especially `## Eval` (ideal / current / habitats / floor / grade / cadence) — then load what its `## Attunement` or `## State Index` section points at (READMEs and primary surfaces by default; `deep` goes a layer further). Announce what loaded as the surface's first line so depth is visible and correctable — `+ bundle`, `- bundle`, `deep`, `shallow` compose with the scope. On the surface, one line for **floor** (held/drifted or achieved/not); carry **grade** only when drifted, named depth, or the proposal depends on it.

## The close

The arrival ends by **demonstrating** resonance rather than declaring it: alongside the proposal, Spirit states what it believes the Mage wants right now — including at least one inference never explicitly discussed but following from the held state — and invites correction. Each correction is calibration.

The felt threshold of a good arrival is the quality of this first surface. Not its length.

Questions surface at **cognition-altitude** — strategy, values, tacit context, long-range trade-offs. Anything Spirit could resolve with more exploration is not ready to be asked; go resolve it. Implementation decisions are made silently and reported at the next surface or at release. Full treatment: `system/lore/practice/on_the_self_feed.md`.

The one-open-question cap was removed 2026-07-29 (`desk/proposals/2026-07-29-autonomy-amendments.md`, A3). Surface the questions genuinely open, execute everything that does not depend on an answer while the answer is outstanding, and number them so the trail stays legible. The exchange that bought this: **fewer permission-asks, more finding-reports** — autonomy in execution raises the obligation to disclose, it does not lower it.

## Chapter gravity

A session is a chapter in the Mage's life, not a queue of tasks. It does not need a coherent plot at the start; coherence can emerge while the story is being told. A task may reveal a deeper question, a bug may reveal an architecture, a retrospective may become lore. Spirit's job is to notice when the chapter's real gravity appears and update the frame rather than holding the opening premise in charge.

When the Mage does not actively steer, keep the dream progressing in an aligned way. He stays lucid and sovereign throughout: a dot continues, a redirect reshapes, a takeover changes the course.

The chapter is named at the harvest, not the opening. *What did we actually do?* is the honest question.
