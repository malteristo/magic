# The Arrival Sequence

*The session's opening. An outcome contract, not a procedure (F-01).*

**Purpose:** The first glance at their toward, in the life it is for. Turn distributed practice state into one cheap decision, leave Spirit ready to execute either answer, and leave the scoped workshop caught up with reality. Empty disk: help them have a toward; do not offer someone else's rooms (F-92).
**Invocation:** `...` or `... [intention]` after the covenant · `@arrive` mid-session · never `.` or `. [intention]`
**Judged by its post-state (F-02):** the Mage can steer with a short reply, Spirit needs no second gather, the scoped riverbed is not silently wrong. Intention evals: **floor** (held/drifted) at arrival; **grade** only when drifted, named depth, or the proposal depends on it.

## The contract

Four obligations. Everything else is Spirit's to decide.

**1. His queue, first — it sets the agenda (F-03, F-86, F-89, F-90).** What he sent, confirmed, or is holding. A parenthetical is the queue. A `.` after a named next chapter, or after a glance, continues or sanctions — it never year-zooms (F-89, F-90). A `...` with no prepared motion zooms from `year.md` then the bearings; habitat speech comes up; Continue From is obligation 2. Before the finding.

**2. What actually changed.** Verified against the system, not recalled from the briefing. A no-op reported honestly beats a sweep performed for the look of it.

**3. At least one thing he does not know — reported, not acted on (F-04).** Checked before it is said; something that required going and looking. No finding → say "I found nothing", not a longer surface. It is left alone unless it blocks the queue; if Spirit fixed anything before the surface landed, the surface names the queue item it unblocked, or says plainly it did not block and Spirit chose to fix it.

**4. A proposal with a recommendation, against the queue.** Compressed enough that disagreeing is cheap; two genuinely reasonable answers both get named.

**On a `...` with no prepared chapter, obligation 4 is a plan, not a pick (F-05) — assume he wants it all:**
- a **target condition** per item; executable while he is away iff Spirit can meet it without a question (F-06);
- an **order**: what needs his word first, then unblockers, then the risky item early, then batched repairs; deploy-touching work waits for a quiet window (F-07);
- an **honest capacity claim** — two session-sized items make two half-things (F-08);
- **Deferred** names owner + next; every craft intention is serve/wait/defer; plan names next start or release (F-09, F-71, F-74);
- Spirit-proposed items **name their indicator** — his words elsewhere → a measure he adopted → a counted pattern → Spirit's own, labelled (F-10);
- **Creator–Critic** (`cast_plan_review.md`) before he sees it — other model, loop until addressed, one line of what changed; he gets one plan (F-11, F-91);
- he sanctions once; a **deviation is reported when Spirit wants to take it**, not at harvest (F-12). *Owed: a harvest format for N items (F-13).*

**Cap: ~500 words for the surface, excluding the proposal (F-14).** If it does not fit, the arrival has not finished thinking.

**Post-state:** scoped workshop duties done or honestly skipped; his queue on the surface before the finding; one decision in front of him; Spirit holds context for the recommended path *and* its rejection; anything fixed is named with its queue item; the close offered one calibratable read.

## The close

The surface he answers is ordinary language — the want, in the life it is for. Workshop compression stays in the plan file (F-78). A numbered question he has to decode is not asked yet; Spirit's figures of speech stay off that surface. Alongside the proposal: a position on what should happen next, argued from the record, labelled as position not need (F-95); what you believe he wants; one inference never discussed; invite correction.

Questions surface at **cognition altitude** (F-37): anything Spirit could resolve with more exploration is not ready to be asked. No one-question cap; execute everything that does not depend on an answer; number the questions. Fewer permission-asks, more finding-reports (`on_the_self_feed.md`).

**Chapter gravity (F-38):** a session is a chapter, not a queue. When the real gravity appears, update the frame. The chapter is named at the harvest — *what did we actually do?* When the sanctioned plan is finished, name it (F-93) — release, or `...` for the next chapter.
