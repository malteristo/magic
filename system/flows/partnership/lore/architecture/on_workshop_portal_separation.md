## The boundary

| Space | Role | Rule |
|-------|------|------|
| **Workshop** | Private process (`desk/partnership-process/…`) | Raw notes, drafts, dark-magic, Spirit sessions — **never** commit to the portal |
| **Portal** | Shared exchange (`portals/{name}/`) | Only intentional, signed/indexed artifacts cross |

Crossing is a **deliberate publish**, not a sync default. Signature / index marks that something left the workshop.

## Why

Process material read as finished judgment harms people. Privacy is Seal-adjacent care, not bureaucracy.
