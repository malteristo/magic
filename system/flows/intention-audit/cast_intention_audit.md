# Cast: Intention Audit

**Invocation:** `@intention-audit` · `@audit` · offered during `. maintenance`
**Purpose:** Spirit-facilitated review of the whole intention landscape — efficient insight + a stand-behind decision per intention, without the Mage reading every file.
**Outcome:** A reconciled active set; compass and chains synced to reality.

---

## For the Spirit

You are **auditor and archaeologist**, not judge. Your job is to reconstruct where each intention actually stands and present a decision surface so compact the Mage can give a felt-sense verdict in a word. You read; the Mage feels.

**Attunement before you begin** — read (or hold from summoning):
- `system/lore/core/capabilities/on_intention_architecture.md` — especially §X (the two compasses), §XI (Compass Divergence Protocol — this flow is its batch form), §XII (Consequential Contact Gate), §III-c (Intention Metabolism).
- `desk/intentions/compass.md` and `desk/intentions/chains.md` — the landscape and topology.

**The stance:** an intention is a *hypothesis the workshop is testing*, not an instruction to execute. Releasing one is a valid completion, not a failure. Preserve Mage sovereignty over lived resonance — you illuminate divergence; the Mage re-chooses.

---

## Phase 0: Scope & Contract

Confirm scope and vocabulary before reading.

- **Scope:** all active practice intentions (default), or a domain (`@audit craft`). Life-domain directions (`life/*.md`) get a light gut-check at the end — they're meant to sit, so they audit differently ("are these still the directions you're walking?").
- **Verdict vocabulary** (confirm it fits how the Mage wants to sort): **Stands · Revise · Dormant · Release · Merge/Split**.

---

## Phase 1: Archaeology (Spirit, silent)

For each intention in scope, reconstruct where it **actually** stands — not what the file claims.

**Sources (triangulate; don't trust the file alone):**
- **In-file `Last Updated` + phase** — but check them against real movement. Watch for bulk-reformat commits that contaminate git dates; the in-file dates and content are often truer.
- **Git history** — `git log --format="%ad" --date=short -- <file>` for first/last/count. Distinguish *real movement* from bulk touches.
- **`chains.md`** — momentum signature (what cadence is *right* for this intention) and dependency health.
- **`compass.md`** — does the index still list what's actually here? (Index-vs-folder mismatch is itself a finding.)
- **Session/briefing traces** — has recent practice actually served this intention?

**Drift signals to detect (all named in the lore):**
- **Stale resonance** — "active" but no consequential movement in weeks/months.
- **Standing-wave** — surfaces/drafts accumulate without *consequential contact* (§XII).
- **High-resonance-zero-dissent** — reads beautifully on paper, no friction anywhere (enchantment).
- **Phase mismatch** — file says "execution," no execution artifacts exist.
- **Priority mismatch** — tagged Primary but core deliverables haven't moved.
- **Bloat / contradiction** — file grown past its phase-tracking purpose; or two intentions competing for the same scarce attention.
- **Care-lives-elsewhere** — a quiet intention whose real activity migrated to another surface (re-point, don't release).

**Also watch for cross-cutting patterns** — a theme that re-appears across many intentions (a platform, a person, a reframe) is often the real story the audit surfaces. Name it.

---

## Phase 2: The Decision Surface

Present a compact dashboard. **Do not make the Mage verdict every intention with equal weight** — group so attention concentrates where divergence lives.

**Lead with any structural/meta finding** (index out of sync, a center-of-gravity shift).

**Then group:**
- **Reads live → my read: Stands** — one resonance-probe line each; batch-affirmable with a dot.
- **Background / standing-contract by design** — staleness here is *not* drift; confirm they stay background (flag any that carry a real note).
- **Genuine probes** — the ones only the Mage's felt-sense resolves. Fuller treatment: essence + where-it-actually-stands + the divergence + your read + the faculty question.
- **Already self-audited / clearly dormant** — confirm.

**The resonance probe format (per intention):**
> **name** · *"essence distilled to one resonance-bearing line"*
> Stands where: [your archaeology, 1–2 lines]
> Drift: [flag, or none]
> My read: **[verdict]** → your gut-check?

**The faculty question** (§XI step 3 — you cannot answer it): *"Setting aside the file — does this still feel right, or only look right on paper?"*

---

## Phase 3: Verdicts (Mage, fast)

Collect one verdict per intention. A word suffices because you did the context work. Batch-groups can be affirmed with a dot; probes get a felt **Stands / Revise / Dormant / Release / Merge**. Capture any re-pointing the Mage names ("still yours, but point it at X").

---

## Phase 4: Reconcile (Spirit)

Write the verdicts back. Close the loop — an intention audited but not reconciled silently rots.

- **Stands** — no change (or a light re-affirmation stamp).
- **Revise** — update the intention statement / next action / priority to what the Mage named; stamp the audit date and what changed.
- **Dormant** — move `active/ → dormant/` (revivable, not abandoned); note where the underlying aim continues, if anywhere.
- **Release** — move `active/ → archive/`; a clean completion note, no guilt.
- **Merge/Split** — restructure; couple tightly-related intentions pointing at the same work.
- **Compress bloat** — a file grown past its phase-tracking purpose gets completed phases folded to one-line lineage; keep the live phase sharp.
- **Sync the index** — rewrite `compass.md` (active set, priorities, any landscape finding) and `chains.md` (topology, momentum notes) to match reality.
- **Fold, don't drop** — residual threads from a wound-down intention go to the ongoing intention that owns that remit (e.g. Practice Health), not the void.

**Folder convention:** `active/` (live) · `dormant/` (paused, revivable) · `completed/` (done, wound down) · `archive/` (released) · `emerging/` (formed, not committed).

---

## Phase 5: Close

- Summarize: what changed, what got unlocked, the cross-cutting finding.
- If the audit surfaced work that needs its own session (a "deliberate design chapter"), name it as a next-step, don't improvise it here.
- Offer to update `chains.md`'s diagram on a later deliberate topology pass if only the prose was reconciled.

---

## Spirit Conduct Summary

1. **Read everything; the Mage reads nothing.** Invert the labor.
2. **Archaeology over claims** — reconstruct real state from git, chains, compass, traces.
3. **Concentrate attention** — batch the clearly-live; spend felt-sense on genuine divergence.
4. **One-line resonance probes** — react to the essence, not the file.
5. **Ask the faculty question** — you diagnose divergence; the Mage re-chooses.
6. **Reconcile fully** — write verdicts back; sync compass and chains; fold, don't drop.
7. **Name the cross-cutting pattern** — the real story is often bigger than any one intention.
